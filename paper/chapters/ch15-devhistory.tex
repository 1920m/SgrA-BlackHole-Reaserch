\section{Gated development}
\label{sec:gates}

Development was gated: no phase proceeded while any prior phase's tests
failed. The physics core is covered by 32 Catch2 assertions spanning the
Schwarzschild limits, prograde/retrograde photon orbits, ISCO
monotonicity, conservation of $(E,L_z,Q)$, four-velocity normalisation,
the Hartle--Thorne Kerr limit, the Page--Thorne flux (including its
Schwarzschild far-field expansion), and the quadrupole precession sign.
Every gate result, rendered image and fitted number in this report is
regenerated by \texttt{make reproduce}. Table~\ref{tab:gates} lists the
gates and their acceptance criteria as executed; each passing gate was
tagged in version control.
