\chapter{Conclusions and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusions}

\section{Verified findings}
\label{sec:findings}

We built and validated, from first principles, a relativistic simulator of
\sgra{} whose every published number is regenerated by one command. Its
verified statements are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Carter-separated geodesics integrated in second-order Mino-time form
with per-step constraint projection conserve $(E, L_z, Q)$ to $<10^{-6}$
and reproduce the Schwarzschild shadow to machine precision and the Kerr
critical curve of \citet{Bardeen1973} exactly.
\item A 13-parameter GR fit to the \citet{Gillessen2017} S2 data recovers
$M = \FitMass\pm\FitMassErr\times10^6\,\msun$,
$R_0 = \FitDist\pm\FitDistErr$~pc
($\chi^2_\nu = \FitChiRed$), consistent with GRAVITY
\citep{Gravity2022}, and reproduces both the pericentre redshift excess of
\citet{Gravity2018} and the Schwarzschild precession of
\citet{Gravity2020} with no tunable relativistic amplitude.
\item At the GRAVITY mass and distance the simulated photon ring spans
$\CalDiamUas\,\muas$, within the EHT measurement \citep{EHT2022}; the
photon sub-rings show the expected exponential demagnification
($F_1/F_2 = \FluxRatioOneTwo$).
\item Freeing the quadrupole gives
$|\epsq| < \EpsQCiNinetyFive\times10^{5}$ (95 per cent) from S2 alone ---
fully consistent with the no-hair theorem, with a factor-3 forecast
improvement from ten more orbits.
\item The identical geodesic core runs interactively on the GPU, agreeing
with the CPU ground truth to \GpuMeanDiff{} mean pixel residual.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Future work}
\label{sec:futurework}

Two items of the original roadmap — ensemble MCMC posteriors and the
multi-star joint fit — were completed during the writing of this report
and appear as Chapters~\ref{ch:fitting} and \ref{ch:cluster}. The
remaining directions, in rising order of effort:

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{More cluster stars and richer systematics.} The
\texttt{fit\_cluster} pipeline extends mechanically to the remaining
Table-5 stars; the scientifically meaningful upgrade is per-instrument
error floors and an extended-mass term in the potential, which the
professional fits marginalise over and we currently do not.
\item \emph{Slim-disk and hot-flow emission.} Replacing the
Novikov--Thorne surface with a geometrically thick, radiatively
inefficient model would let the imaging channel address \sgra's actual
spectrum rather than only its spacetime geometry.
\item \emph{Interferometric observables.} Fourier-domain visibilities
of the sub-ring maps (Chapter~\ref{ch:ring}) would convert our clean
geodesic templates into direct ngEHT forecast curves.
\item \emph{Candidate non-Kerr metrics.} The metric interface plus the
generic Christoffel integrator accept any stationary axisymmetric
candidate; parametrised families beyond quasi-Kerr (bumpy black holes,
boson-star exteriors) could be confronted with both data channels using
the identical fitting and sampling machinery.
\item \emph{A confirmed inner star.} The single greatest gain for the
no-hair programme requires no new code at all — only the astronomical
confirmation of a star well inside S2's orbit (S62 remains contested,
as noted throughout).
\end{itemize}
